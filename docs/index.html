Rebet signal tracker

Paste signals from Discord

Paste as many signals as you like, straight from Discord. Review what I found, fix anything, then add.

Data is saved only in this browser.

Pending and placed bets are assumed lost 36 hours after the estimated game end unless a matching pasted win post has updated it. Checked while open and when reopened; no live score or Discord monitoring. Skipped bets are excluded. You can correct results or reopen a bet to keep it manual.

Alerts only fire while this page is open in a tab (browser limitation). Keep it pinned and you'll get a notification + banner when a bet is within its reminder window.
